Bags swinging, mitts cracking, the room moving as one. Your trial isn't a watered-down taster, it's a genuine coached session, scaled to exactly where you are.

What happens next

From tap to mats.

  1. Step 1

    You send your details. Name, number, and whether mornings or evenings suit you better.

  2. Step 2

    We call you back. Within a few minutes, a quick 10-minute chat about your goals so we slot you into the right session.

  3. Step 3

    You train, free. A full coached session: warm-up, technique, pads and bag work, at your own pace, wraps provided.

  4. Step 4

    You decide. Like it? We'll show you the plan that fits. No pressure, no obligation, you've lost nothing but an hour.
