Money questions

Straight answers.

Is there a joining fee?

Yes, a one-time ₹1,500 joining fee, and it's waived entirely on the 6-cycle and annual (13-cycle) terms. Commit for the longer block and it disappears.

Can I pay monthly, or do EMI?

Yes. We take UPI and card, and monthly (4-week) billing is always available. The 6-Week Fight Camp can be split into 2 × ₹5,250 or 3 × ₹3,666.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. For genuine reasons like travel, illness or a work crunch, we'll freeze your cycle. Because we bill in 4-week blocks rather than calendar months, a pause simply shifts your next start date; you never lose paid time.

Do you offer refunds?

Your first session is free, so you try before you buy. Beyond that, the Fight Camp carries the “Win Your Camp Back” guarantee: do the work and if you don't hit the target, your next six weeks are free until you do. That's a stronger promise than a refund. It's a promise of the result.

Why do you bill every 4 weeks instead of monthly?

So your payment always lands on the same weekday and your training blocks line up cleanly. It's how most combat gyms worldwide bill, and it keeps everything predictable for you.
