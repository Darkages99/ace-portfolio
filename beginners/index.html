Every worry, answered

The things you're actually thinking.

I'm too unfit to start

Nobody arrives fit — that's the reason they arrive. We scale every round to the level in the room, and the level in a beginner class is beginner.

I'll be the worst one there

Groups are capped at eight and beginners start together. There's no back row to hide in and no advanced class watching you.

I'll get punched in the face

No sparring in your first six weeks — for anyone. You build technique and fitness first. Contact is always optional and always your call.

I'll get injured

Every session is coached at 1:8 and progressive. We warm up properly, teach form before intensity, and a coach is watching you the whole time.

I always quit

That's why we build in accountability — a private group, coach check-ins, and a six-week finish line with a results guarantee so there's a reason to keep showing up.

I don't know what to wear or bring

Comfortable clothes, water, a towel. We provide wraps for your first session. There's a one-page “your first day” note the moment you book.

Minute by minute

What actually happens in your first class.

No mystery, no being thrown in the deep end. Here's the whole hour, start to finish.

  1. 0–5 min

    Welcome & wraps. A coach meets you, wraps your hands, and asks what you want out of it. No forms to fill mid-session, no cold start.

  2. 5–15 min

    Warm-up. Skipping, mobility and light footwork to get moving. Everyone goes at their own pace — you are never the one holding the room up.

  3. 15–35 min

    Technique. One or two fundamentals — a jab, a guard, how to stand. Broken down slowly, drilled on pads or in the air, corrected by the coach.

  4. 35–50 min

    Rounds on the bag & pads. You put it together at your own intensity. This is where the fitness happens and where it starts to feel genuinely fun.

  5. 50–60 min

    Cool-down & a plan. Stretch, breathe, and a quick word on what to work on next — plus your nutrition chart if you're joining.

Beginner questions

Still wondering?

Am I too old to start?

No. We coach all ages and levels, and every round is scaled. Plenty of our members started in their 30s, 40s and beyond, with no athletic background at all.

What if I've got an old injury?

Tell the coach before class and we'll work around it. Coached training at 1:8 means someone is always watching your form and can adapt movements on the spot.

Do I have to spar?

Never in the first six weeks, and never against your wishes after that. Many members train for the fitness and technique and choose not to spar at all — that's completely fine.

How quickly will I see results?

You'll feel fitter and sleep better within the first couple of weeks. On the Fight Camp we measure body composition at weeks 0, 3 and 6 so you get a real, tracked number at the halfway point — not just a feeling.

Do I need my own gloves?

Not to start. We provide wraps and all shared equipment for your first session. Once you're training regularly you'll want your own wraps and gloves, and we'll point you to the right ones.
