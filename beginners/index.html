Most people don't hesitate over the price or the time. If anything holds them back, it's usually a quiet what if. So here are plain answers to the ones we hear most, in case any of them sound familiar.

Every what-if, answered

Things you might be thinking.

What if I'm too unfit?

Nobody arrives fit, that's the reason they arrive. We scale every round to the level in the room, and the level in a beginner class is beginner.

What if I'm the worst one there?

Groups are capped at eight and beginners start together. There's no back row to hide in and no advanced class watching you.

Will I get punched in the face?

No sparring in your first six weeks, for anyone. You build technique and fitness first. Contact is always optional and always your call.

What if I get injured?

Every session is coached at 1:8 and progressive. We warm up properly, teach form before intensity, and a coach is watching you the whole time.

What if I quit again?

It's why we build in accountability: a private group, coach check-ins, and a six-week finish line with a results guarantee, so there's a reason to keep showing up.

What do I even wear?

Comfortable clothes, water, a towel. We provide wraps for your first session, and a one-page “your first day” note lands the moment you book.

Minute by minute

Your first class, start to finish.

No mystery, no being thrown in the deep end. Here's the whole hour, moment by moment, exactly as it plays out.

  1. 0–5 min

    Welcome & wraps

    A coach meets you at the door, wraps your hands, and asks what you want out of it. No forms mid-session, no cold start.

  2. 5–15 min

    Warm-up

    Skipping, mobility and light footwork to get moving. Everyone goes at their own pace, you are never the one holding the room up.

  3. 15–35 min

    Technique

    One or two fundamentals: a jab, a guard, how to stand. Broken down slowly, drilled on pads or in the air, corrected by the coach.

  4. 35–50 min

    Rounds on the bag & pads

    You put it together at your own intensity. This is where the fitness happens, and where it starts to feel genuinely fun.

  5. 50–60 min

    Cool-down & a plan

    Stretch, breathe, and a quick word on what to work on next, plus your nutrition chart if you're joining.

Beginner questions

Still wondering?

Am I too old to start?

No. We coach all ages and levels, and every round is scaled. Plenty of our members started in their 30s, 40s and beyond, with no athletic background at all.

What if I've got an old injury?

Tell the coach before class and we'll work around it. Coached training at 1:8 means someone is always watching your form and can adapt movements on the spot.

Do I have to spar?

Never in the first six weeks, and never against your wishes after that. Many members train for the fitness and technique and choose not to spar at all, and that's completely fine.

How quickly will I see results?

You'll feel fitter and sleep better within the first couple of weeks. On the Fight Camp we measure body composition at weeks 0, 3 and 6 so you get a real, tracked number at the halfway point, not just a feeling.

Do I need my own gloves?

Not to start. We provide wraps and all shared equipment for your first session. Once you're training regularly you'll want your own wraps and gloves, and we'll point you to the right ones.
