Getting here

Parking & directions.

Is there parking?

Yes, there's parking right by the gym on Ashoka Street for both two-wheelers and cars. If you're driving in for the first time, message us and we'll guide you to the nearest spot.

Which is the nearest landmark?

We're in Abiramapuram, just off the main Alwarpet stretch, minutes from Mylapore and T. Nagar. Drop a message and we'll send a live pin.

What are your timings?

Open seven days a week, in two windows: 6:00–9:30 AM and 5:30–9:00 PM. Pick whichever fits your day.

Can I just drop in to see the place?

Absolutely. Come by during class hours, watch a session, meet the coaches. Better still, book a free trial and train while you're here.
